An assistant that actually does the work.

How I use an AI assistant every day - on a chat app, with no new tools to learn - for monitoring, research, errands and documents.

01The idea

Message it like a person. It goes and gets it done.

Instinct lives in a normal chat thread. I send text or a voice note. It reads, searches, browses, writes and reports back - and it keeps working in the background when I am not looking.

02Daily use

Six things it handles for me

Quiet monitoring

Scheduled scans of job feeds and a city tech-event radar, saved into a structured database. It messages me only when something new shows up.

Chat-to-to-do capture

I send a quick message and it becomes a clean, structured to-do entry. No app switching, no retyping.

Deep research

Source-cited documents that label every claim verified or unverified. Recent topics: AI tools meeting design software, a fintech loan product, map-data APIs.

Price comparison

Same product checked across platforms, with cashback routing and card or gift-card offers layered in to find the real lowest price.

Errands, end to end

Groceries, ticket research, and explanations on demand - like working through credit-card EMI math live in chat.

Docs and decks

Briefs, tables and presentations built on request. This page was made the same way: one message, one file.
